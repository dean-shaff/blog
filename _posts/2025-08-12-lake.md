This weekend I went to the lake with some friends. It was totally delightful. We spent the afternoon at [Rangsdorfer See](https://maps.app.goo.gl/crZFK3VtY6vCCvX29?g_st=ipc). I rode my bike from Kreuzberg while everyone else made the smarter decision to take my friend's car. We had beer, salad, chicken nuggets, two dogs, and a ball to play around with! Someone made a pasta salad[^pasta-salad] that was alarmingly delicious. 

The bike ride to the lake was okay -- I was hoping for more gravel trails through the woods, but I mostly found myself on those annoying brick cycleways that go alongside mildly busy yet narrow roads. Luckily the weather was amazing -- not too hot and not too cool -- so I wasn't drenched in sweat when I arrived. 

Once a little tipsy, we frolicked with a great deal of gusto in the shallow lake (it reminded me a lot of the [FKK beach at Müggelsee](https://maps.app.goo.gl/w6RciZLF259KFHC6A)), sometimes passing the ball between us, other times playing keepy-aways. One dog dutifully did a few laps; the other stayed adamantly on the shore, barking heartily whenever he say his momma leap for the ball. 

Not sure what else to mention; I just wanted to celebrate a lovely afternoon spent with beautiful people!


[^pasta-salad]: I usually _hate_ pasta salad. I have these terrible memories of Church potlucks where someone would bring in some ice-cold, overcooked rigatoni smothered in sickly sweet Italian dressing. This was of course complimented by hurried, massive slices of red onions and, dare I say it, _diced red bell peppers_. I still don't like raw red peppers.
